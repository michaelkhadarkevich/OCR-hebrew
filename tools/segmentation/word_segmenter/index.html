Word Segmenter Auto v3

Recommended: choose a dataset folder containing matching PNG/JPG + TXT files.
Automatic mode: with PNG/JPG + TXT pairs, the app can cut every line into words automatically using low-ink vertical gaps.
Manual correction: left-drag to add a box, resize with handles, or delete a wrong box.
RTL order: boxes are ordered right-to-left on the same line.
Auto labels: labels are assigned from the line TXT in Hebrew right-to-left order. Spaces before punctuation do not create fake words.
Punctuation safety: standalone punctuation is attached to a neighboring word when possible. Punctuation-only or unsafe crops are not saved.
Edit: select a box and edit its word if needed.
Resize: drag the small square handles on the selected box.
Delete: select a box and press Tab, or click Delete word box.
Save: writes one cropped PNG + one TXT for every word box.
